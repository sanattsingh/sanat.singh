Guruji Astro

THE ₹1 CAMPAIGN / AROUND 2022

The central idea was simple: a low introductory price could reduce hesitation and encourage people to try astrology-related services for the first time.

During my involvement as Social Media Manager, this offer-led campaign became a significant initiative in the brand’s growth phase.

The opportunity

Make initial access more approachable by reducing the perceived commitment of trying the service.

The communication

The surviving banner puts the ₹1 offer beside the brand’s “Your Life Coach For Life” positioning.

The outcome

A significant campaign, as recalled from my time with the brand. Historical performance figures are not currently available.
