## Naming decisions and asset limitations

- **CGX Medical** is confirmed by the supplied logo; not CJX Medical.
- **Surgery Tours India** is confirmed by the supplied logo; not Surgery Tools India.
- **Serene Communities by Columbia Pacific** is one brand context, not two engagements.
- `pharmassist glow tablet.webp` visibly shows the **Hair** product. Its destination and caption say Hair. A standalone Glow creative was not supplied; Glow appears in the product-range image.
- `knauf logo.webp` includes a baked-in checkerboard. It is retained but unused. `assets/brands/knauf-india.svg` comes from the authentic SVG URL already referenced by V1: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/2c/KNAUF_Logo_2024.svg
- No standalone Kohinoor Dark Rum or Shubham Graphics logo was supplied: their names use text wordmarks. Kohinoor artwork remains authentic.
- The Guruji banner is 738 × 225; the two Serene creatives are 399 × 501. Their original resolution limits sharpness at larger sizes. All creative images link to the full-size local asset for inspection.
- The old MagniClipper product image, old Pharmassist hero and old extracted Pharmassist logo are retained but not displayed. Original PNG counterparts of the five reused WebP images are retained for recovery, not downloaded by the page.
- Proxima Nova is not supplied. CSS declares it first and uses the approved Avenir Next / Helvetica Neue / Arial fallback. Playfair Display regular and italic are self-hosted as Latin WOFF2 files (43.7 KB combined), with the SIL license included.
